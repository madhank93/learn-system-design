## The learning loop

**See** the concept → **Play** with a live simulation in your browser → **Break it**: `just syslings` boots the real Go + Nginx stack in Docker with a deliberately broken config. Fix the file, save, and the check re-runs against live traffic until it goes green → **Go deeper** into the mechanics and trade-offs.
